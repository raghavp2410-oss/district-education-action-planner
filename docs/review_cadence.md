# Quarterly Review Cadence

## Cycle

| Step | Timing | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Re-run bottom-quartile flagging query | Week 1 of quarter | Data/analytics |
| 2. Assign field verification to action owners | Week 1–2 | Program coordinator |
| 3. Field verification visits + checklist completion | Week 2–8 | Field verification team |
| 4. Log outcomes, confirm/revise figures | Week 8–10 | Data/analytics |
| 5. Closure review — confirm flagged issues addressed | Week 10–12 | Program coordinator |

## Target

**90% of flagged issues reviewed per cycle.**

"Reviewed" means a completed field verification checklist exists for the
district-indicator pair, with either (a) figures confirmed accurate, or
(b) an action owner and target closure date assigned. A district carried
over unreviewed into the next cycle counts against this quarter's target,
not the next one.

## Tracking

| Metric | How it's measured |
|---|---|
| % flagged issues reviewed | completed checklists / total districts flagged that quarter |
| Carryover rate | districts flagged in consecutive quarters (signals a stalled action owner, not just persistent low infra) |
| Confirmed-accurate vs. revised rate | tracks whether reported UDISE+ data is systematically over- or under-stating ground conditions |

## Escalation

Any district flagged for 3 consecutive quarters without closure is escalated
to the next review level regardless of action-owner status — recurring
bottom-quartile placement after multiple verification cycles signals a
structural issue (budget, staffing) rather than a reporting gap.
