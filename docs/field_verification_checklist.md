# Field Verification Checklist — Bottom-Quartile Districts

Used for every district flagged by
[`sql/02_bottom_quartile_flagging.sql`](../sql/02_bottom_quartile_flagging.sql).
One checklist per district per quarterly review cycle.

## District: _________________  |  Review cycle: Q___ 20___

**Composite score (reported):** _____  |  **Weakest indicator (reported):** _____________

### Verification steps

- [ ] Confirm electricity connectivity at a sample of schools (min. 20% of
      reporting schools or 5 schools, whichever is greater)
- [ ] Confirm functional toilets — specifically check "functional," not just
      "present" (a listed toilet that's locked or broken doesn't count)
- [ ] Confirm drinking water source is operational, not just installed
- [ ] Confirm internet connectivity works at the time of visit, not just
      that a connection exists on paper
- [ ] Confirm boundary wall is intact (not partially collapsed / absent on
      one side)
- [ ] Confirm library has usable books and is accessible to students, not
      locked/storage-only
- [ ] Confirm computer lab has working machines, not just a room labeled
      "computer lab"
- [ ] Confirm handwashing facility has running water, not just a fixture

### Outcome

- [ ] Reported figures confirmed as accurate
- [ ] Reported figures found to overstate actual infrastructure — revise in
      system
- [ ] Action owner assigned for gaps found: _____________
- [ ] Target closure date: _____________
